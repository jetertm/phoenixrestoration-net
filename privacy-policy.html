Privacy Policy – Phoenix Books Sync

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Phoenix Books Sync is a private, internal tool that Phoenix Restoration LLC (Verbena, Alabama) uses only to keep its own QuickBooks Online books. It is not offered to the public or to other companies.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission, the tool connects to Phoenix Restoration LLC's own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's official API. It reads and updates that company's accounting records: accounts, transactions, customers, and reports.

How data is used. The data is used only to categorize, correct, and reconcile Phoenix Restoration LLC's books and to prepare its tax records. It is never sold, shared for marketing, or used for any other purpose.

Storage and security. Access tokens are stored encrypted on a private, access-controlled computer used by the business and are never published. Data is only shared with the company's own accountant for tax preparation, at the owner's direction.

Disconnecting. The owner can disconnect the tool at any time in QuickBooks Online (Settings, then Apps). Disconnecting stops all access right away.
