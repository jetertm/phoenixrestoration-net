About

A local remodeler you can call.

When you call, you get the person responsible for the work. That's how we like it.

The seal says restore, renew, rebuild. That's home remodeling here in Central Alabama.

Phoenix Restoration LLC is a disabled veteran-owned company, run by Jessy Jeter from Verbena. From there we cover ten counties: Chilton, Autauga, Elmore, Shelby, Bibb, Coosa, Dallas, Perry, Lowndes, and Montgomery.

Bathrooms, paint, decks, roofs, and floors — and jobs that need more than one trade. We show up, talk plain, and leave the house better than we found it. Meet the team — Jessy does the work, and Eve keeps the schedule and messages moving.

You'll hear us mention Marbury, Jemison, Thorsby, Maplesville, Prattville, Selma, and other towns inside those counties.
