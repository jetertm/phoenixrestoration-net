About

A local remodeler, owner-operated.

The person who answers the phone is the person responsible for the work. That is the whole point of an owner-operated company.

The seal says restore, renew, rebuild. The work is residential remodeling in Central Alabama — not a franchise, and not a handyman route.

Phoenix Restoration LLC is run by Jessy Jeter. We are based in Verbena and Clanton, in Chilton County, Alabama, and we also keep an Autauga County home base. From there we cover ten counties, county-wide: Chilton, Autauga, Elmore, Shelby, Bibb, Coosa, Dallas, Perry, Lowndes, and Montgomery.

The work is bathrooms, paint and trim, decks and porches, roofs, flooring labor inside a remodel, and renovations that need more than one trade. We show up when we say we will, explain the job in plain language, and leave the house better than we found it.

Towns you will hear us mention — Marbury, Jemison, Thorsby, Maplesville, Prattville, Selma, and others — are places inside those counties. Mentioning a town is not the same as having a shop there.
